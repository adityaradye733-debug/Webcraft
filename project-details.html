VeloxPay Treasury OS

Re-architecting a multi-currency corporate treasury dashboard and flagship SaaS marketing experience for global scale.

Client

VeloxPay Financial Inc.

Industry

B2B Fintech & SaaS

Timeline

12 Weeks

Tech Stack

React, Node.js, AWS, Figma

The Challenge

Legacy Latency & Fragmented UX

VeloxPay's rapid growth across 40+ currencies exposed severe frontend bottlenecks in their legacy dashboard. CFOs experienced 4-second table renders during peak settlement windows, while their marketing website failed to communicate enterprise security credentials.

Our Solution

Unified Design System & Edge Streaming

NEXORA designed a dark-mode-first tokenized Figma design system and engineered a virtualized React web application backed by WebSocket FX feeds, alongside an ultra-fast flagship website with interactive ROI calculators.
